Hover-hunter of the skyline

Kestrel Nights

Swing a tether between towers, open the wing cape and glide over the river. Fly through the amber beacon rings to build a chain. A 3.2 km city, 44 by 44 blocks, at the last hour of light.

Mouse
Look. Click the view to capture it. Right-drag if capture is blocked.
W A S D
Run and steer. Arrow keys also look.
Left click / E
Hold to fire a tether at the crosshair. Release to launch.
Space
Jump. Hold in the air to glide.
Shift
Sprint on the ground. Tap in the air to dash.
C · V · M
Clip mode (no HUD, letterbox). Showreel autopilot. Mute.
Speeds are in km/h and heights in metres above the street. Touch screens get an on-screen stick and buttons.
